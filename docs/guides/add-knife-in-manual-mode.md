# Add a knife manually

Use the **Manual** tab when a knife has no product page to import, such as a secondhand purchase, a gift, a custom build, or an older model. Enter the details you know without a URL. The example below is a limited-edition Vosteed Bellamy with M390 steel and a carbon fiber handle.

## 1. Switch to the Manual tab

Click **Add knife** at the top of the sidebar, then click the **Manual** tab:

![The Scrape URL and Manual tabs, with Manual selected](../images/add-knife-tabs.png)

The URL field is replaced by the full form:

![The Add knife page with the Manual tab open](../images/add-knife-manually-form.png)

The form groups fields into **Identity** (brand/maker, model name, model number, designer, country, and price), **Dimensions** (overall length, blade length, blade thickness, handle length, and weight), **Construction** (blade material, style, coating, handle material, locking mechanism, and hardness), **Notes**, and **Images**.

## 2. Fill in what you know

Enter each measurement with its unit, as stated by the maker:

![The dimension fields, with the Bellamy's measurements entered](../images/add-knife-dimensions.png)

Only a name is required. Leave unknown values blank so the record does not imply that they have been verified. The Bellamy above has no model number or stated country of origin, so both fields stay empty.

## 3. Add images

Under **Images**, paste an image URL into the field and press Enter or click the **+** button; dropping files onto the dashed area works too:

![The image library: the URL field with its + button, and the drop area](../images/add-knife-image-panel.png)

Images are stored in your vault with the record and included in ZIP backups and [cloud backups](../reference/cloud-backup.md). They remain available if the original website removes them.

## 4. Save the knife to your collection

At the bottom right of the form, click **Save Item**:

![The Save Item button at the bottom right of the form](../images/add-knife-save-bar.png)

**Save Item** saves the record and returns you to Collection. You can then edit, compare, or pin the knife, log maintenance, and find it through search or a Smart Collection.

## 5. See the saved knife

Click the new knife's card in **Collection**, then choose **Open full page →** in its preview to review its details:

![The saved Bellamy record: gallery, specifications, and notes](../images/add-knife-manually-saved.png)

## Which route to use

- **Import from a URL** when a product page describes the knife. See [Add a knife from a product URL](add-knife-to-collection.md).
- **Enter it manually** when there is no suitable page, or when you only want to record a few details.
