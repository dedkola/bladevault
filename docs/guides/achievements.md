# Earn achievements

BladeVault awards badges as your collection grows: milestones such as your 100th knife, new makers, steels and countries, care habits, complete records, and lucky finds on [Price Drops](price-drops.md). There are 100 badges in four tiers. Each tier is worth points (Bronze 10, Silver 25, Gold 50, Platinum 100), and your score sets your collector rank, from **Rookie** to **Jedi Collector**.

## When you unlock one

- A message slides in at the top of the screen with the badge, its tier and points. Gold and Platinum badges add a short burst of confetti, unless your system is set to reduce motion.
- The **Achievements** tab in the [Inbox](log-maintenance.md#use-the-inbox) keeps a note of each unlock and the knife it was credited to.
- Badges are permanent. Selling or deleting a knife later does not take one away.

When you first turn achievements on, badges your vault already meets are added quietly, without messages.

## See your trophy case

**Insights** shows a **Trophy case** panel with your rank, the latest badges, and the three closest to unlocking. Open **All achievements** to see every badge with its progress, filter by category or by **Unlocked**, **Locked** and **Secret**, and choose **Share card** to save a picture of your rank and best badges. The card shows no knives, prices or other vault details.

Seven badges are secret. Turn on **Show secret achievements** to list them as “???” until you find them.

## Settings

Open **Settings → Achievements** to turn achievements, on-screen messages, Inbox notes, and secret badges on or off. **Reset…** clears every unlock; badges your vault still meets come back quietly.

Market badges need price monitoring. Money badges count prices in USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, CAD and AUD, and collection-wide badges need at least 10 knives.

In the browser version of BladeVault, achievements last until you close the tab and have no Inbox notes. MCP clients can read your progress with `get_achievements` ([MCP](../reference/mcp.md)).
